How To Play How To Play

Lord Tempus has mixed up all the fairy tales! Help Lumi explore the islands, rebuild the stories, and fill the book of fables.

  1. 1

    Explore the islands

    Travel across the world map and discover different islands, each with a fairy tale to save. Tap waypoints to move around and reach new islands as you progress.

  2. 2

    Complete the chapters

    Enter an island and follow the path: each stop is a chapter of the story. Read the dialogues, find out what happened, and complete every chapter to rebuild that fairy tale.

  3. 3

    Put events in order

    In each chapter, drag the scene cards into the right place, from left to right. Think about what happens before and after — if you're stuck, flip a card to read the clue!

  4. 4

    Unlock the book

    Every chapter you complete is added to your book of fairy tales. Collect them all to break Lord Tempus's spell and bring order back to the world of stories!
